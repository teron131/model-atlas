# Glacier signature

The signature opens with Glacier by default and offers Phase Ledger as the interactive alternative.
Both share the heading, model-role readouts and provider colours.
Glacier keeps light text over its dark film in either page theme, while the surrounding controls follow the page theme.
The flower does not encode model scores or respond to the pointer.

## Assets

- `public/signatures/glacier/glacier-digital-flower.mp4` is the loop-prepared video used at every viewport size.
- `public/signatures/glacier/glacier-digital-flower-poster.webp` is its first-frame fallback.
- `pnpm run signature:glacier` rebuilds only the poster with FFmpeg and Sharp; it never generates or modifies the video.

The selected video was generated by `gemini-omni-flash-preview` from the original orange reference, with the prompt: “Change this original video to Glacier colours: icy blue light and pale blue-white highlights. Keep everything else the same. Make it loop seamlessly. Output 720p.”
The original is 1280 × 720 at 24 fps, with 196 frames and an 8.166667-second duration; its SHA-256 is `26d1aa27453dd63f0883bdf19b1ad012616b545a46de5eb5c72cd316519c92d3`.
The shipped version selects original frames 33–152, from 1.375s to 6.375s, and plays that interval at a constant half speed for a ten-second loop.
The endpoints were selected by comparing five-frame neighbourhoods for matching flower shapes, lighting and particle positions, then visually checking the join for prominent loose leaves.
Motion-compensated intermediate frames maintain 24 fps at half speed; playback stays forward without acceleration, reversal or a dissolve.
The frame neighbourhood around the join has no prominent falling leaves; faint background fragments remain in the source artwork.
The generated source is not exactly periodic, so the selected endpoints are a close visual match rather than identical frames.
The unmodified source is recoverable from Git history; the local working copy is `.cache/glacier-loop/original.mp4`.

Original reference: <https://d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net/user_38xzZboKViGWJOttwIXH07lWA1P/hf_20260808_064556_051587f1-74a1-4336-8c05-4dde3594ed05.mp4>.
Local generation records and reference materials are archived in `.cache/glacier-source-archive/` and are not required to build or run the site.

## Playback and framing

`GlacierMaterial.tsx` owns playback and the poster fallback.
It loads the video only when selected, visible, active and allowed by the reduced-motion preference.
Offscreen and hidden playback pauses; returning resumes it, and switching modes releases the decoder.
Reduced motion, autoplay denial or a media error leaves the poster visible.

Desktop framing contains the video within the right side of the content row.
Narrow browser windows keep the title and model readouts together over the video, with the model rail below.
The content row grows with its text; the video and poster share a crop, and directional scrims preserve text contrast.
