# Glacier signature

The signature opens with Glacier by default and offers Phase Ledger as the interactive alternative.
Both share the heading, model-role readouts and provider colours in the fixed dark presentation.
The flower does not encode model scores or respond to the pointer.

## Assets

- `public/signatures/glacier/glacier-digital-flower.mp4` is the canonical, unmodified video used at every viewport size.
- `public/signatures/glacier/glacier-digital-flower-poster.webp` is its first-frame fallback.
- `pnpm run signature:glacier` rebuilds only the poster with FFmpeg and Sharp; it never generates or modifies the video.

The selected video was generated by `gemini-omni-flash-preview` from the original orange reference, with the prompt: “Change this original video to Glacier colours: icy blue light and pale blue-white highlights. Keep everything else the same. Make it loop seamlessly. Output 720p.”

Native looping preserves the selected clip's existing loop boundary.

## Playback and framing

`GlacierMaterial.tsx` owns playback and the poster fallback.
It loads the video only when selected, visible, active and allowed by the reduced-motion preference.
Offscreen and hidden playback pauses; returning resumes it, and switching modes releases the decoder.
Reduced motion, autoplay denial or a media error leaves the poster visible.

Desktop framing contains the video within the right side of the content row.
Narrow browser windows keep the title and model readouts together over the video, with the model rail below.
The content row grows with its text; the video and poster share a crop, and directional scrims preserve text contrast.
